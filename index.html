Why green roofs

A roof that does more than shed rain.

RAINFALL

Stormwater retention

The substrate and drainage layer buffer heavy rain, easing pressure on drains and reducing runoff into the sewer network.

CLIMATE

Urban cooling

Evapotranspiration from the vegetation layer lowers surface temperatures, cutting the urban heat island effect roof by roof.

ECOLOGY

Habitat & biodiversity

Even a modest Sedum roof gives pollinators, insects and birds a foothold in otherwise sterile rooftop terrain.

ENERGY

Thermal performance

The build-up insulates the deck against summer heat gain and winter heat loss, easing load on HVAC systems below.

DURABILITY

Membrane protection

Shielded from UV and thermal shock, a waterproofing membrane under a green roof typically outlasts an exposed one.

PEOPLE

Usable, calmer space

Intensive build-ups turn dead roof area into gardens, terraces and amenity space that people actually want to use.

How we work

From initial assessment to established greenery.

01

Site assessment

We review the roof or wall, available light, water supply, drainage and maintenance access. Structural capacity and fixing requirements are confirmed with the appropriate project professionals.

02

System design

We select the roof build-up or living wall system, plant palette, irrigation and drainage approach to suit your site, budget and intended use.

03

Installation

Installation is coordinated with waterproofing, structural and service requirements, followed by planting and irrigation checks appropriate to the selected system.

04

Establishment & maintenance

An agreed care plan covers plant establishment, irrigation, drainage, pruning and plant health, with visit frequency based on the system and local conditions.

Planning support

Start with the right questions.

For architects, landscape designers, developers and property owners. A clearer brief makes a better green roof or living wall.

Discuss your project →
01   What should I share for an initial review?

The project location, roof or wall area, drawings or photographs, intended use, construction stage and available water supply. Tell us about any structural or access constraints already identified.

02   How is a suitable system selected?

Selection considers structural capacity, sunlight, wind exposure, drainage, planting goals, budget and maintenance access. Roof loading and wall fixings require review by the relevant project professionals.

03   How do you approach Indian climates?

Plant selection and irrigation planning need to respond to local heat, rainfall, dry periods and site exposure. A planting approach suited to one city may need to change for another.

04   What happens after installation?

Establishment and ongoing care are planned around the chosen system. This includes plant health, irrigation checks, drainage inspection, pruning and replacement needs.

Our story & expertise

Nature belongs in the blueprint.

Across our cities, rooftops and walls hold an overlooked possibility: space for nature to return. Earth Green Life began with a simple belief — greenery should be part of how we design buildings, with its needs considered from the very beginning.

Bringing that belief to life takes more than plants. It takes an understanding of materials, structures, water and the people who will care for a space. Our expertise spans technical textiles and geosynthetics, civil engineering, sourcing and international trade, and strategic client management. Together, these perspectives help us connect the green vision with the practical decisions that make it possible.

We are building an approach to green roofs and living walls rooted in Indian conditions: local climates, individual buildings and realistic maintenance needs. From the first conversation to system planning, installation coordination and ongoing care, our purpose is to help architects, developers and property owners make living greenery a lasting part of the spaces they create.
