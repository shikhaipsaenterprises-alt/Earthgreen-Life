Why green roofs

A roof that does more than shed rain.

RAINFALL

Stormwater retention

The substrate and drainage layer buffer heavy rain, easing pressure on drains and reducing runoff into the sewer network.

CLIMATE

Urban cooling

Evapotranspiration from the vegetation layer lowers surface temperatures, cutting the urban heat island effect roof by roof.

ECOLOGY

Habitat & biodiversity

Even a modest Sedum roof gives pollinators, insects and birds a foothold in otherwise sterile rooftop terrain.

ENERGY

Thermal performance

The build-up insulates the deck against summer heat gain and winter heat loss, easing load on HVAC systems below.

DURABILITY

Membrane protection

Shielded from UV and thermal shock, a waterproofing membrane under a green roof typically outlasts an exposed one.

PEOPLE

Usable, calmer space

Intensive build-ups turn dead roof area into gardens, terraces and amenity space that people actually want to use.

How we work

From bare deck to established roof.

01

Site assessment

We review structural load capacity, slope, drainage points and access, and confirm what the existing roof can carry before anything is specified.

02

System design

Based on the assessment, we spec the build-up — substrate depth, drainage capacity, plant palette — matched to your climate, budget and structure.

03

Installation

Our crews install the waterproofing check, protection and drainage layers, then lay substrate and vegetation, sequenced to keep the site safe and dry.

04

Establishment & maintenance

Scheduled visits through the first growing season and beyond keep coverage, drainage outlets and plant health on track.
